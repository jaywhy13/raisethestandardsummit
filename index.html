A First-Of-Its-Kind Landmark Gathering

From Survival to Structure, Discipline, Ownership & Purpose

A multi-generational gathering bringing together boys, men, educators, faith leaders, business pioneers, and community stakeholders from across the Greater Toronto Area and Ontario. This is not simply an event—it is an intervention to create a sustained framework for collaboration, strengthening pathways to long-term success.

Strategic Focus

Areas of Action

01

Education

School success, literacy, discipline, post-secondary planning, trades, and the future of work.

02

Finance

Financial literacy, budgeting, credit, investing, business ownership, and building generational wealth.

03

Mental Health

Identity, purpose, loneliness, career stress, emotional fatigue, and how men can help each other.

04

Family

Healthy relationships, fatherhood, co-parenting, marriage under pressure, and restoring family bonds.

05

Justice

Community safety, conflict resolution, intervention, reintegration, and mentorship that prevents harm.

06

Health

Fitness, nutrition, preventative care, heart health, diabetes, and healthy aging.
